## 📜 Description

Kara is a confident and composed warrior whose presence on the battlefield is both radiant and commanding. She is quick to help others and is a force for good, showing particular concern for the [[Gryphon|Gryphons]] and her allies. Kara has a [[Kara's Girlfriend|girlfriend]] who appears to be a significant emotional anchor.

---

## 👁️ Appearance

She has flowing tattoos of animals running across her arms, and a weapon that manifests from her own body. Her sword is a large spinal tattoo that flows like water into her arms. She is often accompanied by her shadow-dachshund, [[Maiz]].

---

## 🔮 Spellcasting

- **Divine Smite**: Demonstrated radiant strikes, suggesting paladin or similar divine magic.
- **Teleportation**: Appeared capable of short-range teleportation during combat, often triggered by intimidating foes.

---

## 🧬 Abilities & Traits

- **Tattoo Weaponry**: Her sword is a flowing spinal tattoo that transforms into a weapon.
- **Intimidation-Triggered Teleportation**: Kara can intimidate foes, gaining an advantage that allows her to teleport towards them.

---

## 🧰 Equipment

- **[[Smithing Stones|Fire Smithing Stone]]**: Worn as a necklace, channels fire into attacks.
- **Animated Tattoos**: Tattoos of animals that seem to move across her arms.
