## 📜 Description

Ronin is a swordmaster: disciplined, deadly, and quiet. He acts as the Soul Vessel’s tactical core, surfacing most often when combat requires efficiency, or when Reath traditions must be respected. While not cold, he is distant—observing rather than engaging.

> [!ronin] When [[Kara]] offered me a gold coin, it made me feel recognised and understood. She has earned my blade.

---

## 👁️ Appearance

The same red hair and pale skin as the Vessel, but with a solemn, poised demeanour, and a headband on his forehead. His posture is always straight, his movements precise. He speaks few words—unless speaking in [[Reath - The Old Kingdom|Reath]], in which case he is far more expressive.

### Forehead Gem Colour
Pale yellow

### Shadow Form
In shadow form, Ronin's headband bears a glowing pale yellow rune.
