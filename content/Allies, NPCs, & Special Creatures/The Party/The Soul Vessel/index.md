## 📜 Description

The Soul Vessel is a red-haired humanoid of pale skin and medium build, first encountered by the party suspended in a cocoon within the depths of the [[Silver Canopy]]. Despite being physically trapped, it can project different egos — each with distinct personalities, memories, and capabilities — into the world. These egos take turns inhabiting and animating the body, though they may communicate internally or even manifest briefly under the right conditions.

The Soul Vessel is a magical and spiritual phenomenon, with each ego contributing unique skills, but all sharing a core set of magical abilities and equipment.

As of Session 53, the following egos have been encountered: [[Ryn]], [[Ron]], [[Ronin]], [[Rime]], [[Rain]], and [[Ren]].

## 🧬 Abilities & Traits

- **Projected Ego**: Capable of projecting an active ego outside the body to speak, fight, or interact—even while the physical body is asleep.
- **Shifting Persona**: Changes in speech, posture, knowledge, and fighting style depending on the active ego.
- **Grasping Beam**: Can shoot multiple beams of energy that pull or push foes and harm them.
