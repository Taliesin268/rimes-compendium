## 📜 Description

Ron isn't the most socially graceful of the Soul Vessel's egos, but he is fiercely protective and driven by a sense of justice. He surfaces in moments of anger or when vengeance is needed, embodying the spirit of an avenger. His demeanor is often aggressive, but he fights with a strong sense of purpose.

---

## 👁️ Appearance

Similar to the others in form, but more imposing. His red hair is hidden underneath his helmet and his face scarred. The most notable detail: his glowing red eyes, filled with anger.

### Forehead Gem Colour
Red

### Shadow Form
In shadow form, Ron's helmet has a glowing red rune on the forehead.
