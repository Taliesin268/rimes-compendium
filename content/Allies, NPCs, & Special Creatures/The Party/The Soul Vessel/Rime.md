## 📜 Description

One of the egos within [[Allies, NPCs, & Special Creatures/The Party/The Soul Vessel/index|The Soul Vessel]], Rime is seldom seen, and even less often in control — but he's always observing. He is the one who records, who reflects, and who remembers. While others take the helm for battle, survival, or expression, Rime typically steps forward only when questions need answering — or when something must be written down before it slips away.

---

## 👁️ Appearance

Red-haired, pale-skinned, and thoughtful in demeanor. His hair has more grey streaks than the others, and he has enough facial hair to be considered a beard.

### Forehead Gem Colour
Light blue

### Shadow Form
In shadow form, Rime's book has a glowing blue rune on the cover.

## 🔮 Spellcasting
- [Frostbite](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:frostbite): A cantrip that allows him to create a frigid blast of air.
- [Frozen Orb](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:chromatic-orb): A spell that creates a magical orb of cold energy, which can be hurled at enemies. 
- [Ice Knife](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:ice-knife): A spell that fires a shard of ice that explodes on impact, dealing cold damage to multiple targets.
- [Magic Missile](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:magic-missile): A spell that creates magical darts of force that automatically hit their target.
- [Rime's Binding Ice](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:rimes-binding-ice): A spell that creates a nova of cold energy, freezing enemies in place.
