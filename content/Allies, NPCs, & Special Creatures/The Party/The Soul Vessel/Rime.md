## 📜 Description

One of the egos within [[The Soul Vessel]], Rime is seldom seen, and even less often in control - but he's always observing. He is the one who records, who reflects, and who remembers.

While others take the helm for battle, survival, or expression, Rime typically steps forward only when questions need answering - or when something must be written down before it slips away.
