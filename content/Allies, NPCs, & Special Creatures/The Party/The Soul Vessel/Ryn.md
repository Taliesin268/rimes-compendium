## 📜 Description: Ryn

> [!ryn] Hey, that's me!

**Forehead Gem Colour:** Violet
**Appearance**: A red-haired boy, younger than the others. Freckled, wide-eyed, and gentle in bearing. His expressions are more animated, his voice higher and filled with earnestness.

Ryn is the most innocent and emotionally open of the egos. He is protective, compassionate, and particularly drawn to creatures that others might find monstrous. When the party first encountered the Soul Vessel, it was Ryn who emerged to speak - pleading for help, and fighting bravely beside them.

- **Animal Affinity**: Particularly fond of animals; reacted gleefully to the sight of the [[Maiz]].
  
  > [!ryn] Maiz reminds me of a cat I used to know.
