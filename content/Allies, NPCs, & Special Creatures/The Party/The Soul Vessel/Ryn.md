## 📜 Description

Ryn is the most innocent and emotionally open of the egos. He is protective, compassionate, and particularly drawn to creatures that others might find monstrous. When the party first encountered the Soul Vessel, it was Ryn who emerged to speak—pleading for help, and fighting bravely beside them.

> [!ryn] Hey, that's me!

---

## 👁️ Appearance

A red-haired boy, younger than the others. Freckled, wide-eyed, and gentle in bearing. His expressions are more animated, his voice higher and filled with earnestness.

### Forehead Gem Colour
Violet

### Shadow Form
In shadow form, Ryn's shield has a glowing violet rune.

---

## 🔮 Spellcasting

- Divine Smite: Ryn can channel divine energy to smite foes, dealing radiant damage.

---

## 🧬 Abilities & Traits

- **Animal Affinity**: Particularly fond of animals; reacts gleefully to the sight of creatures like [[Maiz]].
- **Interception**: Can use his shield to protect allies, stepping in front of attacks.

## 🧰 Equipment
