## 📜 Description

Xune is a member of the party, known for her powerful druidic magic and ability to communicate with animals.

---

## 👁️ Appearance

She has long, wavy black hair that frames her face and extends past her shoulders. Her ears are pointed, consistent with elven ancestry. Her eyes are heterochromatic - her left eye is purple, and her right eye is red. A patch of light-colored scales extends from her right cheek down to her neck, forming a distinct contrast with the rest of her skin. She wears layered dark robes and a deep purple cloak. In her left hand, she holds a curved wooden staff. A pale-furred rat rests on her right shoulder.
