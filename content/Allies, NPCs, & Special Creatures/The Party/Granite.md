## 📜 Description

Granite is a dwarven figure of few words and sharp precision. He is deadly in combat, capable of immense damage when he connects, and is known for his silent approach and arsenal of weapons. His primary weapon is a magical bow that conjures radiant arrows and grants him supernatural accuracy.

---

## 👁️ Appearance

Wears a cloak distorted by the shapes of numerous weapons. His magical bow is always close at hand, and he moves quietly, rarely speaking.

---

## 🔮 Spellcasting

- **[Silvery Barbs](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:silvery-barbs)**: Has demonstrated the ability to cast this spell.

## 🧬 Abilities & Traits

- **Unerring Arrow**: Can fire arrows that always strike true, even piercing multiple targets.
- **Silent Movement**: Nearly soundless approach.
- **Precision Strikes**: Capable of immense damage when he connects.

## 🧰 Equipment
