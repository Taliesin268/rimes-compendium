**First Encountered:** [[Session 54 (2025-05-27)]]

One of [[The Kings of Extinction]], Vuela is a shape-shifting entity with powerful druidic abilities. Vuela revives after death with new abilities.

Vuela is mostly unclothed except for furs, and can wildshape into powerful creatures, including a triceratops and a beholder.

## Abilities
- Druidic wildshape (triceratops, beholder)

## Relationships
- Partner: [[Mion]]
- Ally: [[Wildfire]]
