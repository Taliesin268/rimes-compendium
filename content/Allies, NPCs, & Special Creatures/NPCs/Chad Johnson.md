## 📜 Description

Chad Johnson is a legendary bard and a member of [[The Legends]]. He is renowned for his knowledge of the Titans and his role in the defeat of several. Now the owner of [[Glitterstone College]], Chad is preparing for threats to the college and his fiancée, [[Nebula]], while suffering from an ailment that requires him to use a cane. He is a theorist on the separation of body and soul in Titans and has encountered the soul of [[Quake]].

## 👁️ Appearance

A man who walks with a limp and uses a cane. His bearing is dignified, and dresses in the style of a bard.
