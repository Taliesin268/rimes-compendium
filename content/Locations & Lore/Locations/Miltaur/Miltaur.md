## 📜 Description

Miltaur is a continent split into six kingdoms, all of which have been vying for control of one-another for time immemorial. There was a brief respite of peace which ended about one hundred or so years ago. There are groups who live in the air, below ground and on the water.

To travel to Miltaur from any of the other continents requires traversing a harsh desert in the continent's South-East.

[[Allya Adamahn]], a Loxodon war hero who lived out her last days on [[the Serenity Islands]], is dead. She has Ascended to the theretofore unknown [[God of Justice]] as their immortal Acolyte.

As was arranged, [[Metric]] (Allya's lifelong partner and brilliant inventor) moved into the palace after her death. He is mute, and has gained the unfortunate nickname the Crown's Shadow, as he is often seen (but not heard) shuffling through the palace.

Metric is in deeply sorrowful mourning, and communicates very little. He does, however, go out of his way to be polite to the guards and general staff of the palace.

Before Allya's death, the [[Deck of Souls]] was reunited. In doing so, many hundreds of people who Allya had supposedly killed in war were brought out of their temporal slumber and appeared on the Serenity Islands.

[[Saber Eldenblood]] had been defeated and [[the Champions of Allya]] agreed to succeed Allya in her duties when she died. These developments rendered the Serenity Islands finally safe. With this, alongside the completion of the Deck of Souls, Allya's last wish was finally granted: to save the very souls she was tasked to annihilate.

The completion of her quest, thanks to the substantial aid of the Champions of Allya, is what allowed her to ascend into an Acolyte. She watches over the city and islands to this day.

Below Metric and Allya's house, on the holy southernmost island, is [[the Progenitor's Cage]]: a trio of automatons created by Metric who collectively cage the [[Serene (Race)|Serene]] [[deity of Joy and Suffering]].

They told the Champions that [[a Warforged girl]] (who was born, not made) is destined to come to the Serenity Islands, and will deal with the caged god once and for all.

## 🌀 Other Signs

- **[[Dhosaw]]**: Unaffiliated travellers. A full circle drawn with a straight arm, beginning and ending on the opposite side of your body.
- **[[Orra]]**: Royalty or Leaders, or their procession. Originally the sign of the All-Chosen, but it's been adopted by all leaders. A hand on the heart, the other thumb drawing a line across the forehead, signifying purity and the crown.
