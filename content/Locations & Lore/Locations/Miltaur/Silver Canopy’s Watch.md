## 📜 Description

Silver Canopy’s Watch is a settlement or outpost situated near the borders of the [[Silver Canopy]]. It serves as a forward base for monitoring and responding to the canopy’s expansion. The adventuring party originated from this location before venturing into the Canopy itself.

> [!rime] While I haven't personally observed the Watch, its function seems both military and investigative.

The existence of this Watch suggests organized resistance or research efforts against the canopy’s spread, and indicates that the threat it poses is well known to at least some factions or regions of [[Miltaur]].
