
## 📜 Description

"Hollow" refers to a malady or condition observed in certain creatures, most notably the elder gryphon in [[Session 53 (2025-05-20)]] and the entity known as the [[Husk]]. Creatures afflicted by this malady are left emptied—hollowed out—of their essence, vitality, or soul, resulting in a shell that is neither truly alive nor undead.

## 🧠 Observed Symptoms & Effects
- Victims appear gaunt, pallid, and emotionless, with a lack of will or agency.
- Upon death, hollowed creatures often collapse into dust, leaving no remains.
- The elder gryphon, after being infested by [[Emberling|Emberlings]], began to resemble a Husk, suggesting a direct link between Emberling infestation and the hollow condition.

## 🔗 Relationship to Emberlings & Emberachnids
- [[Emberachnid|Emberachnids]] are not themselves hollow, but act as constructs or carriers for Emberlings.
- [[Emberling|Emberlings]] infest living creatures, hollowing them out from within and causing the hollow malady.
- Emberachnids bear a physical resemblance to hollowed creatures, but their true nature is as constructs or drones, not victims of the malady.

## 📝 Notable Cases
- The elder [[Gryphon]] in Session 53, after Emberling infestation.
- The creature known as the [[Husk]], which displays all the classic symptoms of being hollowed.
