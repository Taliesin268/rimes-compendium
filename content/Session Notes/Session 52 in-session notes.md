The Serene (they/them)
Speaks by singing / humming / whistling - but that's only with fellow Serene. For us plebs, they can project images into our minds. When they tried to project an image to me, it was like "firing a cannonball into a whirlpool of ships - they had a glancing blow". I suggested that with practice, maybe they'll be able to project directly to one of the egos.

They sends an image of scales (the weighing tool) as a way of advertising when she thinks a decision's is morally or ethically incorrect to make.

They also seems to favour tolling the dead.

They used some sort of powerful ability that required everyone in 30ft. to make a con saving throw or get blasted by thunder and radiant damage.

They can also fly, swim, and walk - though walking is slowest because they're a sneeehk

Oh, and they're a cleric.

When I met them, their scales were black and shimmery, like some kind of Obsidian. It seems to me like this is subject to change.

Kara (she/her)
I believe she's a Paladin, she definitely did a divine smite at one point. She has a shadow-dachshund called "Maiz".

She has a girlfriend that seems to be a powerful bond for her.

She also seems to have a strong connection to helping others. She did so for me without hesitating, and seemed particularly perturbed by the Griphon.

She also seemed to be able to teleport.

She has a bunch of moving tattoos on her arms, particularly of animals just hanging out. Her sword is actually a large spinal tattoo that flows like water and into her arms to manifest.

Zeke (he/him)
"Hi, I'm Ezekial Corvis, and I'm going to take over the world."

Seems to be a self-reportedly squishy wizard-type. He seems to be able to fly slowly - although he didn't have much magic available in the fights that I've witnessed. Keen to see what he can do.

I've heard rumours he's a were-raven, but yet to see it manifest. Perhaps that's how he took flight?

He looks like a senile old man. I think of him like the guy from DotAGE.
