# 📓 Session Notes

This folder contains raw records, summaries, and in-world reflections from each session of play. These notes serve as the factual backbone of the compendium, informing [[Chronicles/index|Chronicles]], character entries, and lore development.

Unlike the more stylized [[Chronicles/index|Chronicles]], these are primarily meant to document what happened, who was present, and what was learned - even if not all of it is remembered correctly.
