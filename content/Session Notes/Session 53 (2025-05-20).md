The session kicked off in a battle against 12 [[Emberachnid|Emberachnids]]. The [[Gryphon|gryphons]] were on the opposite side of the battlefield, and we were trying to save them. [[Xune]] opened the session with a [Cloudkill](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:cloudkill) spell, which immediately tore a hole in the [[Emberachnid|Emberachnids]]'s ranks. 

[[Xune]], [[Granite]], [[Ezekial Corvis|Zeke]], and the echo of [[Ronin]] all focused on carving a path through the [[Emberachnid|Emberachnids]], while [[The Serene]] and [[Ryn]] teleported to the Gryphons side, and focused on healing and protecting them.

During the battle, we learned more about the [[Emberachnid|Emberachnids]].
1. They appeared to be untroubled by their soullessness. Instead, they appeared to be unburdened of the weight of a soul.
2. We already knew they were resistant to fire from [[Session 52 (2025-05-13)|Session 52]], but we learned that they are also resistant to slashing and piercing (even magical). [[Ryn]] also realised they seemed more susceptible to cold damage.

[[Allies, NPCs, & Special Creatures/The Party/The Soul Vessel/index|The Soul Vessel]] learned more about [[Granite]]'s abilities during the fight. He seems to have a magical arrow that always fires true, even if it has to pass through other creature to meet its mark. He's also capable of some level of spellcasting, as observed by him casting [Silvery Barbs](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:silvery-barbs).

The battle was won without too much trouble. [Cloudkill](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:cloudkill) continued to wipe out spiders like a cloud of bug spray, and between [[Ryn]]'s shielding and [[The Serene]]'s healing, the [[Gryphon|gryphons]] ended the battle better than they started it.

When only one [[Emberachnid]] remained, [[Xune]] attempted to communicate with it using speak with animals. While [[Xune]] was able to understand the creature's spider-like tongue, it's as if it spoke with a heavy accent, tinged with primordial influence.

With one final taunt from the [[Emberachnid]], the party put it down, and turned their attention to the [[Gryphon|gryphons]]. Much like themselves - the [[Gryphon|gryphons]] were covered with [[Emberling|Emberlings]]. While the party turned their attention to clearing the [[Emberling|Emberlings]] from the [[Gryphon]]'s bodies, we discovered the elder [[Gryphon]] had been partially hollowed out by the creatures, and now resembled the other creatures we had seen that had been attacked by the [[Emberling|Emberlings]], such as the [[Husk]].

With none of the party having access to the [Regenerate](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:regenerate) spell, the party turned to more creative ways of healing the [[Gryphon]]. While [[Granite]], [[Ezekial Corvis|Zeke]], and the echo of [[Ronin]] all went to scout ahead, [[Xune]], [[The Serene]], and [[Ryn]] stayed behind - though [[Ryn]] wasn't of much help as he had his eyes and ears relaying through [[Ronin]].

[[Xune]] & [[The Serene]] came up with the idea of turning the [[Gryphon]]'s [flesh to stone](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:flesh-to-stone), so that they would be able to [mend](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:mending) it. While the juvenile [[Gryphon]] was less than pleased with the plan - it didn't object, and allowed [[Xune]] to proceed with the spell.

The process was successful. Although it didn't restore the limb to its full condition, the [[Gryphon]] was able to stand again - albeit a little more stiffly.

In the meantime, [[Granite]], [[Ezekial Corvis|Zeke]], and the echo of [[Ronin]] had scouted about 15 minutes ahead. Something they noticed is that when one [[Emberachnid]] stopped and suddenly sharply changed directions, so did another. Implying that they must have some kind of connected thoughts. Whether that's a hive mind between the two of them, or whether they're controlled by another entity.

[[Ryn]] swapped his consciousness to [[Ronin]]'s echo - disappearing from [[Xune]] & [[The Serene]]'s side. He then manifested [[Rain]], as she would be more effective at long range. However, the extra range was not needed. [[Granite]] being the deadly assassin that he is was able to completely eradicate anything he targeted before they even threatened to approach the group.

As [[Granite]] picked off some wandering [[Emberachnid|Emberachnids]], we observed some peculiar behaviour: It seemed like [[Emberachnid|Emberachnids]] that were near enough to one another would instantly noticed one being assassinated, whereas [[Emberachnid|Emberachnids]] that were a little further away would ignore the situation completely.

After picking off a few [[Emberachnid|Emberachnids]]; [[Granite]], [[Ezekial Corvis|Zeke]], and [[Rain]] decided to return to the others so that we could regroup before progressing any further into [[Silver Canopy|the canopy]].

After regrouping, the party decided to rest. [[Xune]] [molded earth](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:mold-earth) to form a protective barrier around the area where we camped. [[Ezekial Corvis|Zeke]] conjured a [[Gryphon]] to provide a familiar comfort to the two [[Gryphon|gryphons]] as they recovered.

As the party rested, [[Ronin]] kept watch the whole night, with [[Xune]], [[The Serene]], and [[Kara]] taking turns watching alongside him. Overnight, there were very few interruptions. A [[Hollow]] [[Troll]] searched around our area, but didn't find us. 

Right before everyone woke up, an [[Emberachnid]] started searching cracks in the ground, trying to suss out our location. [[Ronin]] - having witnessed [[Granite]] brutal efficiency when fighting from ambush - woke him up, and asked him to take out the [[Emberachnid]]. He did so silently and efficiently.

Before we set off for adventure, we left the [[Gryphon|gryphons]] in the care of [[Ace]] so that he can take them back to [[Silver Canopy’s Watch]]. As a parting gift, he lent us [[Ace's Crow|a magical crow]], which upon landing on [[Ezekial Corvis|Zeke]]'s shoulder granted him the ability to see in the dark. During this exchange, the topic of [[Capitalist Nerd's Bonded Rings|Zeke's ring]] - an item stolen from a capitalist nerd some time in the past. [[Ace]] revealed that he had managed to get his hands on the other ring belonging to said nerd. 

When they brought the rings close together a small portal opened up, revealing a scroll which [[Xune]] identified as a [[scroll of Thaea's Thunder]], with [[Thaea]] being a herald of the apocalypse, also known as [[Thaea|The Black Sun]].

The session ended with the party fully rested, and ready to continue their hunt for the [[Bestiary/The Titans/index|Titan]] [[Wildfire]].
