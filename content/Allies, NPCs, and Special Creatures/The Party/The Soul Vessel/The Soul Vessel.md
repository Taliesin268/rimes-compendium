## 📜 Description

> [!ryn] Hey! That's us!

The Soul Vessel is a red-haired humanoid of pale skin and medium build, first encountered by the party suspended in a cocoon within the depths of the [[Silver Canopy]]. Despite being physically trapped, he projected an ego - Ryn - into the material world, assisting the party in battle and requesting their help in freeing his body.

The Soul Vessel is not a singular entity, but rather a shared conduit for multiple egos, each possessing distinct personalities, memories, and capabilities. These egos take turns inhabiting and animating the body, though they may communicate internally or even manifest briefly under the right conditions.

This phenomenon appears to be magical, spiritual, or perhaps both. It remains unclear whether the egos are past lives, splintered identities, or something else entirely.

As of Session 52, the following egos have been encountered: [[Ryn]], [[The One With Red Eyes]], and [[Ronin]]. Others may exist, but have not yet been revealed to the party.

---

## 🧠 Known Abilities

- **Projected Ego**: Capable of projecting an active ego outside the body to speak, fight, or interact - even while the physical body is asleep.
- **Shifting Persona**: Changes in speech, posture, knowledge, and fighting style depending on the active ego.
- **Grasping Beam:** They can shoot multiple beams of energy that pull foes towards him and harm them.
