## 📜 Description

Granite is a dwarven figure of few words and sharp precision. He wears a cloak distorted by the shapes of numerous weapons. His primary armament appears to be a mechanical hand crossbow integrated into his forearm.

In combat, Granite was deadly when he struck true - capable of dealing significant damage in a single blow - though early encounters also revealed occasional misfires and poor luck. Nonetheless, his potential lethality is unquestionable.

---

## 🧠 Known Traits

- **Mechanical Weaponry**: Possesses a wrist-mounted crossbow or similar device.

- **Silent Movement**: Rarely speaks; his approach is nearly soundless.

- **Weapon Arsenal**: Cloak conceals a wide variety of tools and armaments.

- **Precision Strikes**: Capable of immense damage when he connects.
