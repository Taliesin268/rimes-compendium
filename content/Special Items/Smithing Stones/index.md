## 📜 Description

Smithing Stones are magical augmentations that can be embedded into weapons or armour to alter the properties of the equipment they are attuned to. They require a skilled smith to install and attune properly and cannot be swapped out without a similar process.

> [!rime] These stones appear to be part of the party’s standard armament, suggesting a common magical crafting practice or shared technology among adventurers in the region.
