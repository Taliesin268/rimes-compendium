## 📜 Description

A viscous, swirling red smithing stone that absorbs blood mist in the air and returns it to the attacker, functioning much like a vampire’s stone. Identified by [[Rime]] as a Vampiric Smithing Stone.

---

## 🧠 Known Properties

- Absorbs blood mist
- Returns vitality to the attacker (vampiric effect)
