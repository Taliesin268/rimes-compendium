
**First Observed Use**: [[Session 52 (2025-04-13)]]

---

## 📜 Description

Smithing Stones are magical augmentations that can be embedded into weapons or armour to alter the properties of the equipment they are attuned to. 

> [!rime] These stones appear to be part of the party’s standard armament, suggesting a common magical crafting practice or shared technology among adventurers in the region.

---

## 🧠 Known Properties

- **Damage Transmutation**: Kara’s Smithing Stone altered her weapon’s damage to fire.

- **Swappable Augments**: Kara removed the stone mid-combat upon discovering the [[Emberachnid|Emberachnids]] were resistant to fire, suggesting the stones can be added or removed quickly and without ritual.

---

## 💎 Known Variants

- **Fire Smithing Stone**: Changes the damage type of the user’s weapon to fire. Observed on [[Kara]] as a pendant.
