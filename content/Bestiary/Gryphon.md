## 📜 Description

Gryphons are majestic, aggressive beasts with the head, wings, and forelimbs of an eagle and the hindquarters of a lion. They are proud and territorial creatures often found in mountainous or forested regions, where they establish nests on high cliffs or among the canopy.

They are carnivorous predators, capable of hunting large game and defending their territory with ferocity. Despite their aggression, they are intelligent enough to form bonds - occasionally with humanoids, though more often with their own kind.

---

## 🧠 Known Traits

- **Flight**: Powerful wings allow them to fly with agility and speed.

- **Keen Sight**: Excellent visual acuity makes them hard to surprise.

- **Talons and Beak**: Their natural weapons can rend through armor with ease.

- **Territorial Nature**: Gryphons are known to defend their nests fiercely.
