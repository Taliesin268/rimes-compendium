## 📜 Description
https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Vargouille

The Vargouille is a grotesque, flying head with bat-like wings sprouting from either side of its severed neck. Its features are twisted and infernal, with a paralyzing gaze and a haunting screech.

> [!rime] I had heard of these creatures, though this was my first time seeing them. (Successful Nature check)

Vargouilles are **fiendish parasites** — often found in areas tainted by infernal influence or abyssal corruption. Their bite carries a horrific curse: if not treated in time, it can transform a humanoid victim into another Vargouille.

---

## 🧠 Known Traits & Behaviors

- **Flight**: Moves via leathery wings, generally hovering at head height.
- **Paralyzing Screech**: Emits a horrifying scream that can paralyze those who hear it.
- **Infectious Bite**: A bite from a Vargouille risks infecting the victim with a transformative curse.
- **Prefers Isolated Victims**: Often strikes when its prey is separated or vulnerable.
