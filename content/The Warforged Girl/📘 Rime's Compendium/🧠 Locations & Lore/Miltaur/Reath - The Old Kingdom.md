**Known for:** Their connection to the lands and deities of [[Miltaur]]. They are the native people of Miltaur.  
**Sign:** Wiping the ground, as if removing the top layer of soil.  
**Traditions:** _Chyx_ — Offering a gold coin (or something personally valuable) in exchange for total honesty. Also used to ask for forgiveness when forgetting someone's name or stories.
