## 📜 Description

Kara is a confident and composed warrior whose presence on the battlefield is both radiant and commanding. With flowing tattoos of animals running across her arms, and a weapon that manifests from her own body, she gives the impression of someone who you should not mess with.

> [!ron] I respect the way she composes herself.

Though she was initially hesitant of [[Ryn]]'s projection, Kara was quick to aid Ryn in finding [[The Soul Vessel]]. Kara showed particular concern for the [[Gryphon]]s encountered in [[Session 52 (2025-04-13)|Session 52]].

> [!ryn] These two instances lead me to believe that Kara is a force for good in this world. I'm glad to call her an ally.
